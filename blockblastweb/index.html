Features

One game, three ways to read it

Every move the game makes is expressed visually, audibly and physically at the same time — so losing any one channel still leaves the board completely playable.

  • Three ways to play

    Drag a piece, or tap it and tap a cell, or let dwell control place it for you after a pause you choose. All three drive the same game, and you can switch mid-run.

  • Hear the board

    Every cell has a pitch for its row and a place in space for its column. Scan with a finger and the board plays back as sound — clears sparkle, combos stack harmonics, a bad move buzzes.

  • See the pattern

    Colour is never the only signal. All seven blocks carry a distinct pattern — dots, stripes, crosshatch, waves — plus high contrast, six themes and live colour-blindness simulation.

Accessibility

Built into the model, not the settings page

Playable with the screen off

Every cell and tray slot is a labelled VoiceOver element: “Row 3, column 5, red with dots, fits here, clears 2 lines.” Audio Explore turns the board into a direct-touch surface — drag to hear each cell, lift to hear it described, triple-tap to place. It works with Screen Curtain on.

Readable without colour vision

Seven colours, seven patterns, always drawn. Deuteranopia, protanopia, tritanopia and achromatopsia simulation apply to the whole game rather than a preview swatch, and the Minimal theme paints every block the same white — proof the game never leans on hue.

No gesture you cannot make

Dragging is never required. Dwell control commits a move after a pause you set between 0.4 and 4 seconds and works with Switch Control. Near-misses snap to a legal spot, undo is earned continuously, and Zen mode has no game over at all.

Themes

Six ways to look at it

Every theme keeps the patterns. Choose the one your eyes prefer — including one built purely for contrast, and one that removes colour from the equation entirely.

  • Classic

    Bright, saturated blocks.

  • Neon Glow

    Glowing blocks on deep black.

  • Pastel Soft

    Soft, low-intensity colours.

  • High Contrast

    Solid fills, thick borders, maximum separation.

  • Nature

    Wood, stone and leaf tones.

  • Minimal

    One colour. Pattern carries every identity.
